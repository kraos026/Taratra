# Product Vision

Status: **Implemented**

## Mission

Help enterprises understand how they operate, identify explainable improvement opportunities and
translate approved decisions into deterministic automation specifications without surrendering
business control to an LLM.

## Product direction

- V1 Enterprise Intelligence turns validated knowledge into published recommendations.
- V2 Execution Platform turns published recommendations into abstract, versioned automation
  designs and specifications.
- Execution, deployment, monitoring and optimization remain Planned.

## Decision flow

Evidence becomes normalized knowledge. Deterministic engines derive findings and opportunities.
Versioned economic models evaluate ROI. Recommendation selects an explainable roadmap. V2 consumes
only published upstream snapshots and never rewrites V1.
